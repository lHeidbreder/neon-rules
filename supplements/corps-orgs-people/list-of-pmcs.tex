\section*{Vanguard Security Solutions}
\vspace{5mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
%alternatives: vss
\paragraph{Size}
Large and international, employing around 200,000 mercenaries across various divisions. VSS operates in nearly every major corporate city and arcolopolis.
\paragraph{Training and Equipment}
VSS operatives are ex-special forces and top military recruits. They receive high-end training in urban warfare, crowd control, cyber-ops, and psychological warfare. Standard equipment includes reinforced body armor, non-lethal and lethal weapons, and advanced riot gear including protection from chemicals.
\paragraph{Augmentation}
Moderate – most frontline operatives receive one or two simple enhancements such as enhanced senses, night vision or strength-boosting implants.
\paragraph{Operational Style}
VSS operates as a blend of police force and military, specializing in urban pacification and riot suppression. They’re known for aggressive, hands-on control over civilian populations within arcolopolises. VSS is contracted by multiple corporations to handle population management, employing drone surveillance and automated turrets to contain civil unrest quickly.
\paragraph{Ties}
VSS is funded in large parts by Aegis Arcology Solutions and accepts many contracts for them. More importantly they don't accept contracts targeting Aegis and their interests.
\end{multicols}

\section*{IronHawk Tactical Systems}
\vspace{5mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\paragraph{Size}
Medium-sized PMC, with around 60,000 elite operators. They serve primarily as “rapid deployment units”.
\paragraph{Training and Equipment}
IronHawk emphasizes specialized and covert training, recruiting former intelligence operatives and special forces experts in covert infiltration, hacking, and asset extraction. They’re outfitted with advanced exoskeletons, stealth tech, and customizable tactical weaponry.
\paragraph{Augmentation}
High – IronHawk operatives have neural augments that increase reflex time and decrease pain response, as well as internal communications implants for silent coordination. Top-tier mercs get advanced implants to disrupt enemy technology and hack electronic locks or neural networks.
\paragraph{Operational Style}
IronHawk operates in small, autonomous squads, preferring tactical strikes and covert ops over open combat. NeuraVast and Hyperion deploy them on missions requiring stealth, like corporate espionage, asset retrieval, or sabotage of competitor facilities. IronHawk often operates off the grid and with plausible deniability for corporate clients.
\paragraph{Ties}
IronHawk accepts an unusually high number of contracts for NeuraVast and Hyperion.
Both the companies and the PMC's spokespeople have repeatedly denied any direct ties.
\end{multicols}

\section*{Atlas Legion}
\vspace{5mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\paragraph{Size}
One of the largest PMCs in the world, with a force of over 400,000 soldiers. Atlas handles global security needs for mega-corporations, especially in remote areas.
\paragraph{Training and Equipment}
Atlas Legion recruits from global militaries, including veterans from every conflict zone. Their training is brutal and highly regimented, focusing on guerrilla warfare, wilderness survival, and corporate facility protection. They’re outfitted with heavy armor, firearms, and mechanized drones, with field-ready weaponized vehicles for rugged terrains.
\paragraph{Augmentation}
Low to moderate – Atlas operatives receive basic enhancements, like reflex accelerators and basic HUDs (heads-up displays), but focus more on raw strength and firepower than finesse.
\paragraph{Operational Style}
Atlas is the PMC of choice for defending high-value assets like remote research facilities, off-shore rigs, and biotech labs. They favor overwhelming force, with mechanized exoskeletons and drone support. Known for their “scorched earth” approach, Atlas doesn’t shy away from extreme measures to secure their perimeter, and their loyalty to their employers often borders on fanaticism.
\paragraph{Ties}
Due to the requirement of wasteland security operations by corps like XyloGen and Solace, these seek out Atlas' services more often.
These connections appear publicly to be purely pragmatic in nature.
\end{multicols}

\section*{Black Pyre Strike Teams}
\vspace{5mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\paragraph{Size}
Relatively small but hyper-elite, employing at most 10,000 operators globally, including logistics and other support. 
\paragraph{Training and Equipment}
Black Pyre operatives are some of the best-trained mercenaries in the world, focusing on black ops, assassinations, and surgical strikes. They receive specialized training in counter-insurgency, demolitions, and “invisible warfare.” Their gear includes Strike Suits, a variety of weapons for any situation, and devices to scramble enemy electronics and surveillance.
\paragraph{Augmentation}
Extremely high – Black Pyre is known for testing prototype enhancements on its agents, including cognitive augments, adaptive camouflage, and implants allowing for temporary invisibility. Most operatives are so cybernetically modified they’re barely human.
\paragraph{Operational Style}
Black Pyre is feared for its merciless efficiency
	and is typically hired for corporate assassinations,
	high-stakes sabotage,
	or shadow warfare against rival PMCs.
They’re known for leaving no trace,
	taking down high-value targets with chilling precision,
	and often destabilizing entire regions to serve their employers’ goals.
Black Pyre disbands after each contract
	and marks all operatives as \emph{Missing In Action},
	reforming with "new" members based on the next mission’s requirements.
\paragraph{Ties}
Black Pyre has no known permanent ties.
They work exclusively on high-stakes contracts for the highest rollers, and their allegiance shifts frequently depending on the payout.
Loyalty to their contractor, however, is unquestioned.

\end{multicols}
\section*{Asterios Armored Corps}
\vspace{5mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\paragraph{Size}
Moderately sized, with around 80,000 heavily armored personnel and extensive heavy vehicular support. 
\paragraph{Training and Equipment}
Asterios specializes in heavy combat and defense, with operatives trained in mechanized assault and siege tactics. They are outfitted with heavy or semi-powered armor, heavy weaponry, energy shields, and mobile artillery units.
\paragraph{Augmentation}
Moderate – Asterios soldiers have reinforced skeletal structures and pain-dampening implants to withstand extreme physical stress in combat. Their gear includes interface ports for heavy armor systems, allowing them to operate near-indestructible mechs in combat zones.
\paragraph{Operational Style}
Asterios acts as the ultimate blunt force option, deployed for direct confrontations with resistance groups or rival corporations attempting asset seizure. Their standard protocol is to set up heavily fortified positions, using drones and heavy mechs to maintain dominance. They are often called in during active corporate warfare, where high-casualty missions are expected.
\paragraph{Ties}
Asterios is most often contracted to defend infrastructure and frontline corporate interests for clients like Hale Syndicated Finance and Hyperion.
Much like the Atlas Legion, these ties appear publicly to be solely pragmatic in nature, but various personal meetings between board members of Asterios and its clients have cast doubt.
\end{multicols}

\section*{Phalanx International}
\vspace{5mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\paragraph{Size}
Phalanx has a highly specialized team of about 30,000 agents, offering unique services like “executive protection” and high-security transport.
\paragraph{Training and Equipment}
Phalanx operatives are masters of defensive tactics, focusing on executive security, counter-ambush, and cyber-defense. Their gear includes energy barriers, AI-driven defensive drones, and mobile command units. They are well-versed in non-lethal engagement and crowd dispersal tactics.
\paragraph{Augmentation}
Low – Phalanx focuses on mental acuity and leadership skills, using augmentation only where it aids tactical awareness or protection (such as instant threat assessment overlays).
\paragraph{Operational Style}
Phalanx operatives blend seamlessly into corporate environments, providing discrete protection for high-ranking executives and corporate VIPs. Known for their professionalism and restraint, they avoid overt displays of force unless necessary. Phalanx agents employ psychological and digital deterrents to de-escalate situations, making them ideal for guarding high-value personnel without drawing public attention.
\paragraph{Ties}
Phalanx works closely with upper management of any megacorporation, whenever they are in need of safety during public meetings.
\end{multicols}
