While humans are not expected to interact with the Deep directly, some specialists still do.
Colloquially they are referred to as \emph{Divers}, their trip a \emph{Dive}.
\\%
These Divers use heavily modified L1NC implants and other, more powerful equipment to skip the AR Net.
Their bodies go numb, the mind pretends to be just another service in the vast Deep.
\section{The Feeling}
It is an alien world, and very hard to navigate.
First timers have described it as very intense, overwhelming, even distressing - some have a hard time breathing.
Others feel electric, truly alive for the first time, becoming addicted to the experience.
\section{Commercial Use}
The core reason to dive is Net Security.
Skilled Divers can quickly exploit gaps in a service's security measures, gaining easy privileged access.
On the flip side security professionals can find intruders during a dive and kick them out... or much, much worse.
\section{Entertainment Use}
Few people like the feeling.
The ones that do tend to get addicted to the escape from reality.
\\%
Within the Deep there exist a few meeting spots for Divers.
Some just hang out because they are addicted to the feeling, the escape.
Others meet for nefarious deals and exchanges.
Most that dive recreationally find Deep Net equivalents of questionable real world entertainment - drugs, gambling and prostitution.
\\%
Not all dives have to be so dark, however.
The Deep contains all collective human knowledge, if you know where to look, and not all of it is kept under lock.

\section{Into the Hive}
A handful of brave - or foolish - divers have tried entering LEGION's Hive net.
It is easy to find for any seasoned Diver.
\\%
The Diver will hear a single warning, before he enters a network so alien his numb body will begin vomiting.
Should he not turn back, his L1NC will be assailed by LEGION's net itself.
If he can escape, his name will turn up on LEGION's bounty list, and he will be hunted relentlessly.
If he cannot escape in time, his L1NC overheats in a fashion that cannot be replicated by human hands and fries his brain.
