\section*{Pulse}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Vera Santorini
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	Div/Null (formerly)
\paragraph{Known For}
	Pulse is rumored to have executed some of the most devastating cyber-attacks in history, including a corporate “blackout” that allegedly wiped the data banks of a megacorp, erasing years of R\&D, secret contracts, and blackmail material.
	The exact megacorp in question is inconsistent between retellings of the story.
\paragraph{Myths}
	The extent of her abilities and equipment is shrouded in legend.
	After Pulse suddenly vanished, some claim she was taken out by members of the Eclipse Syndicate.
	Others claim she has been fully digitalized, leaving her body behind and wandering the net in form of a ghost AI.

\section*{The Eagle of Solace}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Hawk McMillan
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	Solace MedCorp (formerly)
\paragraph{Known For}
	Hawk McMillan was a notorious “reclamation agent” for Solace, sent to track down and recover stolen biotech.
	He was known as ruthless, often described as \emph{"efficiently brutal"};
	he did, however, have a strict code of honor, refusing to harm innocents and often helping destitute communities with basic medical supplies after his missions.
	Despite working for a corporation, he was a sympathetic figure to many.
\paragraph{Myths}
	Hawk’s legend grew when he allegedly defied Solace and left with valuable biotech secrets.
	Some say he now wanders wastelands and slums, offering “pro-bono” medical aid and training guerilla fighters against megacorp interests.
	\\%
	Every once in a while, someone in the black market shows up with an advanced prosthetic bearing Hawk’s eagle insignia, fueling the rumor that he’s still out there, helping the oppressed.

\section*{Nightmare}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Jaeger Rook (presumed)
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	\begin{sitemize}
		\item NeuraVast (formerly, presumed), IronHawk / Blackpyre (presumed)
		\item LEGION (presumed)
	\end{sitemize}
\paragraph{Known For}
	Barely anything is known about Jaeger Rook.
	He is supposed to be a shadowy, near-legendary, unyieldingly efficient and ruthless.
	He is more than fully augmented, more machine than man, and known for wiping out entire squads of rival PMCs with chilling silence and precision.
	\\%
	To this day, hardened soldiers still shudder at the mention of him and insist Jaeger is just waiting for his next hunt.
\paragraph{Myths}
	Many claim he was actually LEGION’s experiment in mixing organic intelligence with AI, allowing for more covert operations.
	Very little evidence supports these claims.
	\\%
	Many others claim he was a NeuraVast candidate for one or multiple implant prototypes, later joining IronHawk or Blackpyre after either being discarded or fleeing.

\section*{Dr. Hadley}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Dr. Mara Annabeth Hadley
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	Hyperion (formerly), Quiet Dawn
\paragraph{Known For}
	Doctor Mara A. Hadley is a cybernetics and networking specialist, that formerly led multiple research initiatives at Hyperion.
	Their goal was integrating L1NC-friendly, low neural load standards for network communication at increased bandwidths, among others.
	\\%
	When more and more standards were undisclosed to the public, Dr. Hadley defected to the Quiet Dawn, continuing her research with teams there.
\paragraph{Myths}
	According to Quiet Dawn sympathizers, Hadley is a visionary striving to advance humanity beyond corporate control, working on a new net entirely, a cure to corpo-controlled networking.
	\\%
	Hyperion claims she was merely a rogue scientist who defected after failed experiments.

\section*{The Blight}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Ajax Raines
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	\begin{sitemize}
		\item Vanguard Security Solutions (formerly)
		\item Iron Pact
		\item Sons of Aether (possibly)
	\end{sitemize}
\paragraph{Known For}
	Raines was a former corporate soldier turned Iron Pact freedom fighter after he saw firsthand the devastation corporations brought upon war-torn regions.
	His skill with guerrilla tactics and his unrelenting drive against corporate exploitation led him to become a prominent but controversial figure within the Iron Pact.
	\\%
	He is known for weaponizing corporate technologies against their creators, Ajax’s methods often involve contaminating corporate territories, causing a lot of civilian casualties.
\paragraph{Myths}
	Ajax’s most famous exploit is “The Blight of Oshima,” where he allegedly sabotaged an entire megacorp factory arco by lacing its water and air supply with toxins, rendering it uninhabitable.
	Corporate PR insists he didn’t survive the attack, yet insurrectionist cells claim to receive coded intel and strategic tips from “The Blight” himself.
	\\%
	Some say he’s alive but barely human anymore, using stolen implants to survive his toxic methods and haunting the no-man’s lands where corporations fear to tread.

\section*{Spectre}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Lina Kovacs
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	\begin{sitemize}
		\item Eclipse Syndicate (formerly)
		\item Obsidian Eye
	\end{sitemize}
\paragraph{Known For}
	Lina Kovacs, known as Spectre, was once Eclipse’s top infiltrator, skilled in using stealth and manipulation to assassinate corporate and syndicate leaders alike.
	She’s renowned for her ability to vanish, seemingly disappearing from every recording, feed, and prison ever meant to capture.
	\\%
	Her ghostly nickname emerged after she allegedly neutralized an entire PMC squad while they were in a locked, secure room, leaving behind no trace but the carved sigil of an eye.
\paragraph{Myths}
	Spectre’s lore only grew after she was “killed” by a rival assassin, only to resurface years later as a high-ranking intelligence operative for the Obsidian Eye.
	It is unclear whether her death was faked or the Eye operative is just an impostor.
	\\%
	Legends claim she can erase her presence from any digital and biometric systems, and is unnaturally impervious to surveillance, making her the ultimate spy and assassin.

\section*{Seven-Oh}
\paragraph{Actual Name}
	Unknown; All records of his existence have been purged by Black Pyre, aside from his codename \emph{Operative 70}.
	\\%
	An anonymous Div/Null member claims his name used to be \emph{Adam Night}.
	However, said Div/Null member did not publish proof and has gone silent ever since.
\paragraph{Affiliation}
	Black Pyre
\paragraph{Known For}
	Any Black Pyre operative could be considered a legend, but Operative 70 is the only one known by designation.
	His exact deeds are uncertain, but are likely to include protection duties, frontline combat, wetwork.
	\\%
	His name dropped during a Cerberus purge, where a Black Pyre strike team led by him came under fire by heavily armed gangers caught between the infected and the operatives.
	Said gang which opened fire on Black Pyre has seized operations ever since.
\paragraph{Myths}
	Seven-Oh has become a horror story among gangsters and soldiers alike.
	Drill Sergeants claim that, should their recruit identify their targets improperly, Seven-Oh will find them and hunt their squad.
