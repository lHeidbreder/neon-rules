LEGION has its own net, unofficially called \emph{the Hive}.
It is similar to the human Deep Net, and very different.
Instead of connecting to the AR Net it connects to LEGION combat platforms.
\\%
No human is meant to interact with the Hive at all.
Any attempt to breach it has been met with extreme electronic countermeasures, and all who tried have been put on LEGION's shitlist - safe to say, none lived to tell about it.
It does, however, attach to the Deep Net in a few places, allowing for communication with LEGION, however hard it may be to perform.
