\chapter{Briefing - Adjudicator}
\section{Synopsis}
In the sprawling metropolis of \emph{Opportunity},
	the Crew is hired by a mysterious employer to retrieve a stolen AI prototype chip.
Once the plot doesn't add up,
	they finds themselves in the middle of a conspiracy.
And it's their turn to choose a side.
\par
{
	\itshape
	An introductory mission
	in 1 to 3 sessions
	for 3 to 5 players
	passing through the three pillars of roleplaying.
}
\section{The Crew}
It is built for the core rule Prefab Protagonists,
but any Crew may work.
Just make sure that they are decently diverse in skills
and all Protagonists are willing to work together.
\\%
If you use add-on features,
	Protagonists may have abilities to solve conflicts in this mission,
	that are not accounted for.
Be prepared if that is your intention.
\section{On the Usage}
Acts I to III outline a simple mission sequence.
Depending on your needs you can do whatever you like with the information
	- you are the Adjudicator after all.
\\%
The planned time frame leaves very little time for intricacies.
If you want to use the mission for more than a test drive,
	you will have to weave it into the rest of your world.
\\%
These Acts also refer to the content in the \hyperref[ch:index]{Index}.
It may be advisable to skim through it first.
\section{Add-ons}
This mission is built to be played using no add-ons
- You can obviously feel free to add any you'd like to try.
\\%
If this is your first time playing a punishing TTRPG,
it is advised to use \emph{Armor of Fate} (see Narrative Rules, p. \pageref{narrative-ch:armoroffate}).
