\supply{Collective Consciousness Group AP}
	{A multipurpose access point supporting multiple connections in various ways.
		\\%
		The first use-case is allowing a group of people to dive simultaneously.
		The have to access the same service, but can easily find each other within the Deep, allowing inexperienced divers at least some orientation.
		For this use-case it allows up to six simultaneous connections and it has an energy demand of 1 per user, with a minimum of 3.
		\\%
		The second use-case is two divers connecting to each other's consciousness.
		It becomes almost impossible to lie to each other
		- all \emph{Interrogation} tests may be assisted using \emph{\hackingskill} as a free action, in addition to normal assistance.
		Alternatively two divers getting physical with one another can drastically increase the intensity of the experience.
		\\%
		When used in either of these ways, it allows for up to three connections and has an energy demand of 2.}
	{500}
	{5}
