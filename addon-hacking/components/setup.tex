\section{Implants}
To dive a character requires a modified L1NC implant (see p. \pageref{sec:modifiedl1ncs}) to skip AR access.
They are generally hard to procure, and require good connections (such as the \emph{Priviledged Access} boon) to find.

\section{Rigs}
Aside from just implants, characters can use rigs.
They generally consist of a bench or tub, as well as medical equipment like vitals monitors, pain stoppers, temperature control and so on.
They require a decent amount of space and time to set up.
\par
For anyone monitoring energy demand, rigs are often easy to spot, as they require a ton of electricity.
A clever setup can mitigate the energy signature, but it will take additional time to set up or tear down - making for a massive shackle if it is found after all.

\section{Software}
Any diver can prepare code fragments to take with him.
They will speed up certain actions within the deep.

Which fragments and how much code depends on the diver's rig and chrome.
