\section{Special Rules}
\index{Special Rule}
\vspace{10mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\specialrule{Backblast (X)}{Everyone behind the shooter in a cone - X meters in length and X/2 meters in base diameter - takes half the weapon damage as if hit by a weapon with the Blast rule.
	If a wall blocks the cone, the area becomes Blast(X) centered on - and including - the user instead.}
\specialrule{Blast (X)}{Every Subject inside the area - a circle X meters in radius - takes a hit.
	For every 2 meters inside the Blast radius the target is,
	one additional location is hit,
	up to every location once.}
\specialrule{Bulky}{Cannot be carried in holsters, slings or such.}
\specialrule{Cerberus Infection}{When touching an open wound, such as when dealing damage with an attack, the target makes a Constitution test at a -20 penalty. On a failure the target is infected by the Cerberus fungus. Small creatures like mice will simply die.}
\specialrule{Cumbersome}{Halves speed of the wearer/user and grants -10 to all physical actions.}
\specialrule{Disrupt}{Turns off PES for D5 rounds after a hit.}
\specialrule{EMP}{Causes 2D10 rounds of EMP (p. \pageref{hazards:cbrn}).}
\specialrule{Flame}{Sets ablaze any flammable surfaces and objects it hits.}
\specialrule{Flash}{Any affected Subject looking at the source makes an Ins test at -10 or becomes Blinded for DoF+2 rounds.
	In addition any affected Subject within half range makes a Con test at -10 or becomes Deafened for DoF+2 rounds.
	Anyone within a third becomes both Stunned and Deafened.
	\\%
	See p. \pageref{sec:status} for status effects.
}
\specialrule{Flexible}{The weapon cannot be parried. It does not gain the Melee Damage Bonus.}
\specialrule{Improvised}{
	Does not require familiarity,
	but instead always invokes -10 penalty.
	It is also \emph{Unreliable 2}
	and usually breaks apart on a fumble.}
\specialrule{Inconspicuous}{
	The weapon is easier to hide or pass off as something else.
	Depending on the weapon its user gets a +30 bonus to
	either \emph{Sleight of Hand} tests to hide the weapon
	or to \emph{Deception} tests to pass it off as something harmless instead.
}
\specialrule{Light}{
	The equipment is much lighter or otherwise easier to handle than comparative equipment.
	Penalties resulting from encumbrance don't apply, so long as the armor is of the right size.
}
\specialrule{Multitargeting}
	{This weapon cannot perform single attacks;
		it is limited to \emph{Bursts}.
		\\%
		The attack's hits can be split freely among any valid targets,
			so long as they are roughly in the same direction.}
\specialrule{Oversized}{Way too large to be used by humans.}
\specialrule{Piercing}{The user's Melee Damage Bonus is added to the weapon's AP as well.}
\specialrule{Powered}{Requires an external power source to function.}
\specialrule{Protective}{Made for defense: -10 to hit, -2 damage, but +20 to parry.}
\specialrule{Refined (X)}{
	The weapon is made with almost otherworldly craftsmanship, and will most definitely out-shoot you.
	If the weapon is \emph{Unreliable}, reduce its value by X.
	If the weapon is not (or no longer) \emph{Unreliable}, the chance for Critical Hits is increased by X.
	For example, a weapon with \emph{Refined 2} will strike critically on results of 5 or less. %TODO: calculate threshold dynamically?
}
\specialrule{Reliable}{
	Can't Fumble (instead just indicates a miss) and can generally take more of a beating.
	An \emph{Unreliable} weapon cannot be \emph{Reliable}.
}
\specialrule{Rending}{When the attack is blocked by armor (after AP),
	it deals no damage to the target.
	Instead the armor is burnt away
	and reduced by the damage that would have been dealt until it is repaired.
	This will only affect solid armor, not energy shields, nor does it affect natural armor.}
\specialrule{Scatter}{
	Shots at short range or further gain +10 to hit.
	At long range or further the weapon loses all AP.
	Below short range the weapon gains +1d10 dmg.
}
\specialrule{Single Loader}{Reloading may be interrupted after each bullet. The given reload time is a single round of ammunition. \\
	A Reload test - instead of reducing the reload time - increases the amount of shells reloaded during the loading time, but is at a penalty equal to 5 times the reload time.}
\specialrule{Smoke}{The area inside and behind the smoke is fully concealed. Smoke usually remains dense for a minute and lasts for another as slightly obscuring; this will be reduced in wind or active ventilation, or increased in a fully sealed room.}
\specialrule{Spray}{Ignores half of non-sealed armor and all but full cover.}
\specialrule{Sticky}{
	A sticky substance stops the targets' resistance
		without crippling them.
	All targets hit suffer -30 penalties to physical actions
		and have their movement reduced to a quarter.
	\\%
	The substance cannot be removed with water
		but is susceptible to many common solvents.
	The substance can also be removed by
		removing the surface it sticks to
		(e.g. using discardable plates or by undressing).
	Trying to scrape the substance off may be possible
		but will take at least half an hour.
}
\specialrule{Stun (X)}{
	If the attack pierces the target's armor
	and the target fails a constitution check at a penalty equal to 10 times X,
	he becomes Stunned (p. \pageref{sec:status}) for DoF rounds.
}
\specialrule{Tearing}{Roll an additional damage dice and drop the lowest.}
\specialrule{Trauma}{When attacking the head location, halve the target's armor. Also halves Stunning Strike penalties.}
\specialrule{Unreliable X}{
	The weapon is a liability more often than not.
	The weapon's chance of Fumbles is increased by X.
	For example, a weapon with \emph{Unreliable 2} will fumble on results of 96 or higher. %TODO: calculate threshold dynamically?
}
\specialrule{Unwieldy}{The weapon disallows transforming reactions to actions, or vice versa.}
\specialrule{Upgrade Kit (X)}{The weapon is an upgrade kit for another weapon X. Price and weight include X.}
\end{multicols}
