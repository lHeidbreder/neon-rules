\section{Attack-Defense Matrix} %TODO: name properly
\label{attack-defense-matrix}

Attack in head column, Defense in head row.
\par
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{l|XXXX}
	& Crit Fail & Fail & Success & Crit Success \\
	\hline
	Crit Fail & Nothing & Nothing & Small attack advantage & Big defense advantage \\
	Fail & Simple hit & Nothing & Nothing & Small defense advantage \\
	Success & Small attack advantage & Simple hit & Nothing & Nothing \\
	Crit Success & Big attack advantage & Small attack advantage & Simple hit & Nothing
\end{tabularx}


\begin{sitemize}
\item \textbf{Small attack advantage:}
	The attack deals double damage.
\item \textbf{Big attack advantage:}
	The attack deals triple damage.
\vspace{1mm}
\hrule
\vspace{1mm}
\item \textbf{Small defense advantage:}
	The attacker suffers the defender's weapon damage when in melee.
	At range the attacker's weapon jams.
\item \textbf{Big defense advantage:}
	The attacker is disarmed (or, alternatively, knocked prone) and suffers the defender's weapon damage when in melee.
	At range the attacker's weapon jams and cooks off, dealing its damage to the attacker.
\end{sitemize}
