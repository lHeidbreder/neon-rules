\vspace{-10mm}
\section{Status effects}
\label{sec:status}
\vspace{8mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
\statuseffect{Blinded}{
	The Subject cannot see and
	suffers according penalties,
	most notably to Perception and combat,
	or tests may be completely impossible.
	He also cannot \emph{Gather Senses}
	if the condition is only temporary or brand new.
}
\statuseffect{Deafened}{
	The Subject cannot hear.
	She suffers penalties to \emph{Perception}
	or tests may be completely impossible,
	and can't \emph{Gather Senses}
	if the condition is only temporary or brand new.
}
\statuseffect{Frenzied}{The Subject has entered a vicious rage.
	She no longer takes penalties from pain and damage aside from complete destruction of body parts.
	Melee damage bonus is doubled.
	She will throw herself into combat and needs to make Int checks to distinguish friend from foe.
	These checks are at a +30 bonus for very close friends and a -30 penalty for completely unknown people.}
\statuseffect{Jammed}{
	Jams can affect firearms.
	Critical fails while handling firearms usually cause jamming.
	\\%
	Until the user performs a Reload action on the weapon, it cannot shoot.
}
\statuseffect{Nauseous}
	{The Subject gains exhaustion for every minute of strenuous activity.
		In addition, on a failed Con check for any strenuous action, he starts vomiting,
		getting \emph{Stunned} for D5 rounds.}
\statuseffect{Prone}{
	The Subject is granted bonuses and suffers penalties as described in Situational Modifiers.
	If falling prone was involuntary, she also loses D10 initiative.
}
\statuseffect{Stunned}
	{The Subject cannot act except for
		moving with a speed of \sim 1m/s
		and loses 1 initiative for every combat round he is stunned, to a minimum of 0.
		Tests to prevent falling over automatically fail.}
\statuseffect{Unconscious}{The Subject cannot act at all.}
\end{multicols}
