\def\pfcname{Doc Jones}
\subsection{\pfcname}
\subsubsection{Basic Questions}
\begin{enumerate}
	\setlength\itemsep{-8mm}
	\item
		\textbf{Appearance:}
		I'm a scientist and I wear it on my sleeve.
		Because I'm only biologically 22, I had to dye my hair gray and my beard white,
		and I comb my hair up so onlookers would immediately understand my brilliance.
	\item
		\textbf{First Impression:}
		I've been called mad scientist, which of course is preposterous.
		Curious and engaged perhaps, but I digress.
	\item
		\textbf{Childhood:}
		I was grown and educated in a vat.
		They say I didn't come out right but I'll show 'em... I'll show 'em all!
	\item
		\textbf{Past relations:}
		I... never really saw people outside the corp.
		And most patients didn't make it.
		\\%
		As for people currently - I met Awe, when she looked for more supplies.
		And I met the Pack Man fella when I was looking for supplies myself.
	\item
		\textbf{Thoughts on the party:}
		This is the best way to make a name for myself.
		Also, they need my help.
	\item
		\textbf{Traveling:}
		My lab and the ER, really nothing past it.
		I DID however visit a lecture hall once!
	\item
		\textbf{Transhumanism:}
		Very useful, very useful indeed.
		Considering I was purpose-made in the first place,
		why would I not aug every part I can?
	\item
		\textbf{Chems:}
		Makes you work longer and more efficient.
		But my opinion as a medical professional should be clear, right?
	\item
		\textbf{Risking your life:}
		No, no, no.
		The medic dies last, that's just common sense.
	\item
		\textbf{Biggest Wish:}
		I'd love to have my own lab.
		Like, my \textit{own} own.
	\item
		\textbf{Biggest Fear:}
		I have a bad feeling about Golotov.
		He wouldn't just let me go, that's not the type of man he is.
		He probably has some control mechanism left in place and the feeling won't let me go.
	\item
		\textbf{Morality:}
		Yes, yes, it's always about morals.
		But what are morals really if not your upbringing imprinting on your mind?
		So I'm not evil - I'm just different and your argument is invalid.
	\item
		\textbf{Openness:}
		Strangers usually want something from me.
		At best I will be professional, at worst dismissive.
		Depends on how much cash they bring.
	\item
		\textbf{Value of Life:}
		I swore an oath to preserve...
		\textit{however} there is a lot to be learned people suffering and dying,
		and who would let that go to waste?
	\item
		\textbf{Animals:}
		I love animals.
		They are premium test subjects.
	\item
		\textbf{Perspective on Beauty:}
		Beauty is such a strange concept, so superficial.
		So let's say health to get it over with.
	\item
		\textbf{Food and drink:}
		Only the most elegant.
		Like... hot dogs and cheap wine.
		Or tap water.
	\item
		\textbf{Love:}
		I- I'm not good with these flowery mumbo jumbo.
		It's just a nice feeling.
	\item
		\textbf{Dark Secret:}
		I've grown up in a vat.
		What do you think?
		I didn't have the time for a dark secret.
	\item
		\textbf{Defining Traits:}
		I'm a proud medical scientist,
		a bit too invested into surgery.
		I'm also born to be compliant and don't like spending money.
\end{enumerate}
