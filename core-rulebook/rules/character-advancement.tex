\section{Characteristics}
\vspace{4mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
Characteristics can be increased by spending XP,
	up to the characteristic in their \emph{Anthrotype} + 45.
\begin{exampleblock}
	\itshape
	Example:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Pure humans will be able to reach 70 in every characteristic.
		\item Vat-grown Warriors will be able to reach 80 Courage and 65 Charisma.
	\end{itemize}
\end{exampleblock}
The next advancement's cost is dependent on the target value:

\end{multicols}

\section{Skills}
%OLD:
Each characteristic can be increased 5 times by 5 points each by spending XP.
Said advancements cost 200, 400, 800, 1200 and 1600XP respectively,
	making for 4200 in total.
These have to be bought in order for any one given characteristic.
\\%
Lastly, a character may attain \emph{Mastery} of a skill (see p. \pageref{ability-Mastery}).
