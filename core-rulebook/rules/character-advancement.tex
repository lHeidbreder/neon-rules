\section{Characteristics}
\index{Characteristic}
\index{Advancement!Characteristic}
\vspace{4mm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
Characteristics can be increased by spending XP,
	usually up to the characteristic in their \emph{Anthrotype} + 45.
\begin{exampleblock}
	\itshape
	Example:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Pure humans will be able to reach 70 in every characteristic.
		\item Vat-grown Warriors will be able to reach 80 Courage and 65 Charisma.
	\end{itemize}
\end{exampleblock}

\section{Skills}
\index{Skill}
\index{Advancement!Skill}
Skills pass through levels described on p. \pageref{sec:Skills}.
The first increase costs 200 XP,
	the second one (taking a basic skill from known to trained for example) will cost 400,
	the third 600 and so on.
That means increasing a basic skill
	from \emph{untrained} to \emph{mastered}
	will cost 2000 XP
	and taking an advanced skill
	from \emph{unknown} to mastered
	will cost 3000 XP overall.
