\chapter{Armor of Fate}
\label{ch:armoroffate}
Neon is a very lethal game.
Since we don't want our protagonists to die to a stray bullet, they may need to be protected by fate itself. 
This may be represented by luck, unnatural determination and willpower or supernatural intervention
	as per the Adjudicator's discretion and game world.
\begin{exampleblock}
	These rules are a bit more advanced and require a bit more bookkeeping.
\end{exampleblock}

\section{Resource}
At character creation every Protagonist gains \emph{Resilience Tokens} - the amount can range from
\begin{sitemize}
	\item \textbf{1} for a slightly less punishing experience, over
	\item \textbf{2} for heroic legends, to
	\item \textbf{4} for an absolute cakewalk.
\end{sitemize}
Once used they are lost and can be restored by performing heroic acts,
	taking a key role in the Voices' immaculate plan,
	finishing their character arc or other such occasions.
Generally speaking they should be reset at the end of a mission.
\begin{exampleblock}
	Note:
	\begin{itemize}
		\item Don't be too stingy with them, as they are yet another resource allowing the Adjudicator to turn up the heat.
		\item Voices that have few or no tokens can often be given one for less impressive tasks than those who already have a safety net.
		\item Make sure that the key roles - and therefore the Resilience Tokens - don't always fall to the same Protagonists and Voices.
	\end{itemize}
\end{exampleblock}
\section{Uses}
\emph{Resilience Tokens} can be used as follows:
\par
\begin{tabularx}{\columnwidth}{rX}
	1 token & When the Protagonist takes a hit, he takes no damage from said hit. \\
	1 token & When the Protagonist would die, he is stable but comatose instead.
		It is up to the Adjudicator's discretion how long it takes for him to regain consciousness. \\
	1 token & The Protagonist automatically passes a Restraint test to suppress all deterioration as a free action. \\
	1 token & The Protagonist may remove one status effect he is currently suffering from, or end the effects of a chem in his system - with the exception of permanent ones like Luciferium. \\
	2 tokens & The Protagonist becomes immune to pain, fear and similar adverse effects (e.g. getting stunned) for the rest of the encounter.
\end{tabularx}
\par
All of these are subject to the Adjudicator's discretion
	- he may exclude some options, add other ones or adjust the cost
	if the campaign in question deems that necessary.
\begin{exampleblock}
	If you are using add-ons,
	you may use a token
	to prevent or prematurely end
	other debilitating, temporary effects introduced by those add-ons on your Protagonist.
\end{exampleblock}
